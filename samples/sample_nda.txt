MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT 

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into between Northstar Systems, Inc. and Meridian Analytics, LLC. 

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION 

Confidential Information means non-public technical, commercial, financial, or business information disclosed by either party. 

2. CONFIDENTIALITY 

Each party agrees to protect Confidential Information using reasonable care. 

3. PERMITTED USE

Confidential Information may be used solely for evaluating a potential business relationship. 

4. TERM 

This Agreement will remain effective for three years. 

5. GOVERNING LAW 

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Delaware. 

6. ASSIGNMENT 

Neither party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent of the other party.
