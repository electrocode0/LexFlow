# LexFlow Requirments 

## Purpose 

LexFlow is an AI-assisted contract intelligence and review platform designed to transform legal agreements into structured, reviewable data. 

## MVP 

A user can: 

1. upload a contract; 
2. store the contract and its metadata; 
3. extract structured clauses and obligations; 
4. compare provisions against a configurable legal playbook; 
5. surface deviations with supporting source text; 
6. submit results for human review; 
7. maintain an audit trail. 

## Day 1 Scope 

Day 1 establishes: 

- FastAPI backend
- PostgreSQL database
- Docker Compose environemnt 
- initial database schema 
- health endpoint 
- sample NDA 

AI functionality is explicitly out of scope for Day 1.
